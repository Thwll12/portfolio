About Me

I build learning that people actually use, not just complete. Over the past decade, I’ve turned dense software rollouts, compliance mandates, and AI adoption initiatives into training that sticks for organizations ranging from the Department of Defense and the Federal Reserve Board to major healthcare and financial services organizations. At Cybermedia Technologies, that meant designing 15 DoD blended learning solutions that increased participation and enrollment by 25%, and turning firsthand accounts from military subject matter experts into a panel graphic novel, because sometimes even the most technical training benefits from a little storytelling.

More recently, my work has expanded into AI-enabled learning and workforce transformation: helping teams at the Federal Reserve Board adopt new AI tools and using AI-enabled technologies to accelerate development without sacrificing instructional quality, technical accuracy, or accessibility. I do my best work in remote, fast-moving environments where learning strategy can move the needle for employees and the business.
